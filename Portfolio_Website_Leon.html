About Me

Hi, my name is Leon Ibanez Fraile, and I was born in Madrid, Spain. My mother, father, and I moved to the U.S. when I was 13 months old. The vast majority of my extended family still lives in Spain, so we visit every summer. In 2009, four years after I moved to America, my brother Hugo was born. Him and I are pretty different people with very different gifts. Hugo is a very social person, while I’m a very dedicated person. However, we meet with our passion for complex strategy board games, an activity we spend a lot of time doing together. My father comes from a family with a powerful culture around sports. He’s pushed me since I was young to try lots of sports, and since then they’ve become a key part of who I am. I’m not a very mechanically gifted person, so I’ve always practiced less technically demanding sports the most. I’ve been swimming and running pretty much since I was six years old. In part of elementary school and all of middle school, I was on teams for both. Ever since I entered high school, I’ve continued to foster a deep passion for running. I’ve done all three seasons of CRLS athletics since I entered high school. Ever since I made the varsity team on my first race freshman year and I’ve only continued to improve. I’ve become a state-level athlete finishing ninth at the division 1 cross country state meet my junior year, and only continue to improve...

Future Goals

I still have a very broad range of interests and am largely unsure of what majors and careers I want to pursue. Although I’ve been successful and thoroughly enjoyed nearly every class I’ve taken in high school so far, the subjects that have fascinated me the most have been sciences, due to notably AP Chemistry and AP Biology; mathematics, due to mainly AP Calculus BC and AP Statistics; and engineering. Although I still enjoyed and thrived in my humanities classes, based on my most prominent high school interests, the majors I’m interested in exploring throughout college are computational biology, biotechnology, and medicine. These majors would allow me to pursue careers in AI/ML for biology, computational neuroscience, genetic engineering, pharmaceutical biotechnology, and immunology. I have a deep passion for the sciences that I would love to pursue in a career; however, I’m still very open to new subjects and majors and would not be at all surprised if my interests changed. I hope my hard work and dedication throughout high school will allow me to get admitted to a world-class university where I can continue to explore my curiosities and interests.
